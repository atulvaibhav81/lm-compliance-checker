## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a serene, botanical luxury blended with modern editorial clarity. It pairs delicate culinary and natural aesthetics with crisp digital usability. The visual tone is luminous, organic, and composed—evoking fresh pistachio paste, delicate sage leaves, tender spring light, and tailored paper ephemera.

Targeted at premium lifestyle, culinary, conscious commerce, and boutique wellness platforms, the design language leans into an airy, editorial minimalism. Generous white space, soft pistachio washes, subtle tinted borders, and high-elegance typography craft a calming, memorable digital sanctuary.

## Colors

The palette derives directly from lush botanical tones, balancing high-energy spring greens with milky, tender pistachio washes and rich organic contrast:

- **Primary (`#42D674`)**: An energetic, vivacious pistachio green used for primary interactive states, key focal badges, active toggles, and conversion buttons.
- **Secondary (`#BADBA2`)**: A soft sage pistachio that provides soothing structural fills, subtle chip backgrounds, and card strokes.
- **Tertiary (`#E3F0A3`)**: A delicate, creamy chartreuse-tinted pistachio cream, ideal for highlights, ambient tinted containers, and promotional ribbons.
- **Accent Green (`#80EF80`)**: A buoyant mid-tone mint used selectively for hover accents, progress states, and soft glow reflections.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#1B2A1E`)**: An ultra-deep spruce-tinted black that guarantees WCAG AAA accessibility across text and icons while maintaining organic warmth rather than synthetic grey.
- **Neutral Backgrounds**: Canvas levels range from crisp milk white (`#FDFFFC`) to subtle frosted pistachio parchment (`#F4F8F1` and `#EAF2E7`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary elegance of Playfair Display with the functional precision and open geometry of Manrope. 

Headlines command visual curiosity through high stroke contrast, elegant ligatures, and artisanal weight, while body text and data tables remain impeccably legible, neutral, and structurally grounded. All uppercase small labels feature open tracking (`+0.05em` to `+0.08em`) to mirror luxury apothecary and perfumery labeling.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a harmonious 8pt base spacing scale combined with a responsive 12-column grid system for desktop and a 4-column system for mobile.

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, `3rem` (48px) exterior page margins. Max-width content bound at `1320px` to maintain comfortable optical reading lengths.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters, `2rem` (32px) margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `1.25rem` (20px) margins.

Layouts prioritize breathing room: sections are separated by generous vertical rhythm (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`), anchoring content within self-contained, airy cards rather than stacked monolithic stripes.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through soft, botanical ambient light and translucent tinted surface layering, rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas)**: Solid `#FDFFFC` or tinted `#F4F8F1`.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting)**: Pristine `#FFFFFF` resting upon tinted backgrounds, complemented by a hairline border of `rgba(186, 219, 162, 0.45)` and a soft ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(27, 42, 30, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Floating Controls)**: Shadow lifts smoothly: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(27, 42, 30, 0.08)`, border changes to `rgba(66, 214, 116, 0.5)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Drawers)**: Supported by subtle backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with a tint of `rgba(244, 248, 241, 0.85)` and shadow `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(27, 42, 30, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a refined, medium-rounded radius (`0.5rem` base, `1rem` for cards, and full-pill curvature for compact interactive items). This balance avoids child-like bubble aesthetics while banishing rigid, industrial corners, echoing the soft contours of pistachios, seeds, and natural stones.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#42D674` fill with `#1B2A1E` bold label text. Hover state shifts smoothly to `#80EF80` with a soft elevated green aura (`box-shadow: 0 4px 14px rgba(66, 214, 116, 0.4)`). Active state applies a micro-scale of `0.98`.
- **Secondary**: Tinted `#E3F0A3` background with `#1B2A1E` text and a subtle `#BADBA2` border. On hover, transitions to a brighter lime-milk tint.
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Transparent surface, `#1B2A1E` text with subtle underline accent in `#42D674` on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- Rendered as full pills (`border-radius: 9999px`) with generous horizontal padding (`0.75rem`), font size `label-md`.
- Default: `#F4F8F1` fill, `#BADBA2` border, `#1B2A1E` label.
- Highlight / Status: `#E3F0A3` fill with high-contrast `#1B2A1E` text.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Inputs**: Crisp white canvas with a 1px border of `#BADBA2`. Rounded `0.5rem` corners. Placeholder text in muted spruce (`rgba(27, 42, 30, 0.4)`).
- **Focus State**: 2px outline in `#42D674` paired with an ambient tinted glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(66, 214, 116, 0.2)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom checked state uses `#42D674` fill with a crisp white or spruce checkmark, bounded by a soft `#BADBA2` edge when unchecked.

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` or pale `#F9FBF8` background tiles, bounded by `1px solid rgba(186, 219, 162, 0.5)`.
- Header areas feature Playfair Display titles with generous interior spacing (`1.5rem` to `2rem`).
- Micro-interactions include a gentle 2px vertical lift and boundary illumination on hover.
